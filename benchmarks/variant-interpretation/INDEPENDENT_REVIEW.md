# Independent review request

Helena Bioinformatics invites qualified clinical genetics, molecular genetics,
bioinformatics and reproducibility reviewers to inspect the public comparison
protocol for Folklore Clinical Variant Interpretation MCP before comparative
results are collected.

This is a methods review, not a request for endorsement. Reviewers are asked to
identify flaws or omissions in:

1. public-case sampling and stratification;
2. identity resolution and assembly handling;
3. classification- and criterion-concordance definitions;
4. provenance and reproducibility measurements;
5. ambiguity, invalid, unsupported and unavailable outcome handling;
6. conflict-of-interest, patient-data and clinical-safety boundaries.

## How to review

Open a public GitHub issue or pull request against this repository. State your
relevant qualification or project affiliation and any conflict of interest.
Reference the exact section and propose a falsifiable correction or acceptance
criterion. Do not include patient, phenotype, family, segregation or private
case data.

Helena Bioinformatics will classify substantive recommendations as accepted,
accepted with scope, needing evidence, rejected with reason or already
resolved. A publisher-run result will remain labelled publisher-run. No
clinical-accuracy, superiority, diagnosis or treatment claim will be inferred
from this protocol.
